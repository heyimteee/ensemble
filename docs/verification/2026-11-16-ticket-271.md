## Data-retention decisions (implementation owned by #272)

- No time-based expiry for conversations, Work, approvals, memories, bindings,
  or attachments; deletion is explicit (workspace delete or Ensemble reset).
- Logs expire after 7 days, 5 files, or 10 MB per sink by default.
- `AGENTS.md` is project-owned: workspace delete leaves it in place; #272 must
  offer block removal and document the roster disclosure.
- Export must exclude credentials and unrelated private paths; delete must
  cover plugin storage, attachments, the `.ensemble/` files Ensemble created,
  and logs.
- Telemetry: none. No outbound request, analytics, crash reporting, or update
  check exists in `src/v2/`.

## Accepted risks

See `docs/security/accepted-risks.md`. The critical advisories are the
`seroval`/`solid-js` TUI peer chain (`AR-271-01`); the plugin ships no bundled
runtime dependency and `npm audit --omit=dev` is clean. The moderate advisories
are dev-only npm tooling (`AR-271-04`). No high or critical finding is
undocumented.
